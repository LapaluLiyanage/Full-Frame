Privacy Policy

Facebook Album Download Kit · Last updated: September 14, 2026

Facebook Album Download Kit ("the extension") lets you save photos from your own Facebook albums to your computer, either as individual image files or as a ZIP archive. This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with your data.

In short: the extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything happens locally, inside your browser.

What the extension does

  • Reads photo data from the Facebook album page you have open, so it can list and download the photos.
  • Fetches full-resolution photo files directly from Facebook's own content delivery network (fbcdn.net) so they can be saved to your device.
  • Saves your chosen settings (such as image quality and download concurrency) locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect personally identifiable information, health, financial, authentication, or location data.
  • It does not track your browsing activity, keystrokes, or web history.
  • It does not send any data to servers operated by us or by any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking pixels, or remote code of any kind.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties.

Permissions

The extension requests only the browser permissions required to operate on Facebook album pages you open, run its download UI, and save files you choose to download. No permission is used to collect or transmit data off your device.

Data storage

Your extension settings are stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API and are never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the extension removes this data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
